How it works

Your sales history already knows who's slipping away

Most owners can tell you their regulars by name. Nobody can keep track of 800 of them. One More Time does that part.

Step 1 · 10 minutes

Bring your customer list

Export your sales from Square, your booking app, or a spreadsheet and drop it in. We'll do the setup with you on a call if you'd like.

Step 2 · automatic

We learn each customer's rhythm

Some clients come every 4 weeks, others every 10. One More Time sets a personal "due date" for each one and flags them the day they go quiet.

Step 3 · every week

Messages go out, money comes back

A friendly check-in, then a discount, then a last call. You approve the wording once. Every Monday you get a report: who came back and what they spent.

Questions

Things owners ask us

Do I need Square or a special booking system?

No. If you can export your sales or appointments to a spreadsheet, One More Time works. Direct syncing with Square and popular booking apps is on the way.

Will this annoy my customers?

Each customer gets at most three messages per lapse, spaced over three weeks, and never more than one a week. The sequence stops the moment they come back or opt out.

Is texting customers legal?

Yes, when they've agreed to it. One More Time only texts customers who opted in, includes "Reply STOP to opt out" in every text, and respects quiet hours. Customers without text consent get email.

How do I know it's working?

Each customer gets a personal discount code. When they come back, we match the visit to the message and show the revenue in your Monday report.

How big a discount should I offer?

Most owners use 10–20%. The first message has no discount at all, so you only give money away to customers who need a bigger nudge.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account page. Your customer data is yours, and you can download it before you go.
