The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the service, and nothing for advertising.
  • If a business you shop at uses One More Time, that business controls your information. We process it only on their behalf, to send you their messages.
  • We never sell personal information, and we never share phone numbers or text-messaging consent with anyone for their own marketing.
  • Reply STOP to any text, or use the unsubscribe link in any email, and you won't get those messages again.

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how [LEGAL NAME] ("One More Time," "we," "us") handles personal information. It covers two groups of people:

  • Business users: owners and staff who sign up for One More Time, and people who join our waitlist or contact us.
  • Customers of those businesses: people whose information a business uploads or connects so it can send them messages. For this information, the business decides what's collected and why, and we act as its service provider.

2. Information we collect

From business users

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), business name, business type, time zone, mailing address and settings.
  • Billing details: handled by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and a customer ID, never your full card number.
  • Waitlist and contact forms: your name, business, email, phone and anything you write to us.
  • Connections: if you connect Square, we receive an access token (stored encrypted) and read-only access to your customers and payments.

About a business's customers

  • Name, phone number, email address, purchase dates and amounts, whether the person agreed to receive texts, and whether they opted out.
  • Messages sent to them on the business's behalf, and replies they send back.

Automatically

  • A single login cookie that keeps business users signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested), used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit.

3. How we use it

  • To run the service: work out customers' usual visit patterns, send the messages a business has set up, honor opt-outs, count return visits and send reports.
  • To manage accounts, billing and support, and to send service emails such as password resets and weekly reports.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the service, using aggregated information that doesn't identify any person or business.
  • To follow the law, including keeping records of consent and opt-outs.

We don't use a business's customer data to market our own products to those customers, and we don't combine one business's customer data with another's.

4. Text messages

Businesses send texts through One More Time only to customers who have agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies; a typical sequence is up to three messages over three weeks. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver messages for us (see section 5).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and customer data: while the business's account is open, and for up to [90] days after it closes so the business can export it.
  • Message logs and opt-out records: up to [4 years], so businesses can show they followed messaging laws.

7. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hash passwords, encrypt stored Square access tokens, limit login attempts, and keep each business's data separate. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information and will notify affected users of a security breach as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Customers of a business: to see, correct or delete your information, contact the business you shop with; we'll help them respond. You can also email us and we'll pass your request on.
  • Business users: you can update your details in Settings, and ask us to export or delete your account by emailing [PRIVACY EMAIL].

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example, under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act). We'll respond to requests within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for making one.
